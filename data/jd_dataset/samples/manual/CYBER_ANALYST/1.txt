Title: Junior SOC Analyst
Location: Kuala Lumpur

Join our 24x7 Security Operations Centre protecting banking and insurance clients. You will monitor alerts, investigate suspicious activity and help respond to security incidents.

Key responsibilities:
- Monitor and triage alerts in our SIEM (Splunk and QRadar).
- Perform log analysis across firewalls, endpoints and cloud services.
- Support incident response and write clear incident reports.
- Run vulnerability scans with Nessus and track remediation.
- Keep up to date with threat intelligence and the MITRE ATT&CK framework.

Requirements:
- Degree in Cybersecurity, Computer Science or Information Technology.
- Good understanding of networking (TCP/IP, DNS) and network security concepts.
- Working knowledge of Linux and Windows Server.
- CompTIA Security+ or equivalent is preferred.

Advantageous:
- Scripting in Python or Bash.
- Familiarity with ISO 27001 or NIST CSF.
- Experience with Wireshark or penetration testing in a lab or CTF.
