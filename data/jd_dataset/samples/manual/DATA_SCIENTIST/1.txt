Title: Graduate Data Scientist
Location: Petaling Jaya

We are a fintech start-up building credit-risk models for small businesses. As a Graduate Data Scientist you will work with senior scientists to design, train and evaluate machine learning models.

What you will do:
- Explore and clean large transaction datasets using Python, Pandas and NumPy.
- Engineer features and train models with Scikit-learn.
- Evaluate models using precision, recall, F1 and ROC AUC and explain the trade-offs.
- Share results in Jupyter notebooks and short presentations.

What we are looking for:
- Degree in Data Science, Computer Science, Mathematics or Statistics.
- Solid foundation in statistics and machine learning.
- Proficient in Python and SQL.
- Curious, self-driven and able to work in a team.

Preferred:
- Exposure to deep learning with PyTorch or TensorFlow.
- Experience with Apache Spark or cloud platforms is a plus.
